Bookmarklet: save listings with one click

For sites that block the URL fetcher (like Zillow or Redfin), use this instead.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar if it's hidden: ⌘⇧B in Chrome, Edge, Arc, or Safari.
  2. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:
    📌 Save to Tracker
  3. While viewing any apartment listing page, click the bookmark. The listing details get copied to your clipboard.
  4. Come back to this tracker, click + Add Apartment, and paste into the auto-fill box. The fields will populate automatically.
